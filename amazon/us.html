Methodology

How the Index Is Constructed

ACIX is built on a transparent, rules-based framework so that every published value can be traced back to its inputs.

01 / DATA

Signal Collection

We ingest anonymised, aggregated signals from public marketplace pages — rank position, review velocity, price changes, stock status and category depth — refreshed continuously across 21 marketplaces.

02 / WEIGHTING

Composite Weighting

The composite applies fixed weights: Demand 40%, Pricing 25%, Fulfillment 20%, Assortment 15%. Weights are rebalanced quarterly and any change is published in the index changelog.

03 / NORMALISATION

Base & Rebase

All series are normalised to a base value of 100 as of January 2025. Seasonal adjustments use a 24-month trailing decomposition to remove recurring holiday distortion.

04 / GOVERNANCE

Independence

ACIX is an independent analytical publication. Index values are computed from publicly observable signals and are never influenced by advertising, affiliate or commercial relationships.
